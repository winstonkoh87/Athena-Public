# Protocol SG-006: HDB Matrimonial Flat Unwind Firewall

> **Domain**: Singapore Family Law / Public Housing Regulatory Physics  
> **Prime Directive**: Never negotiate matrimonial property division based on gross valuation or informal promises. Every option must pass HDB statutory eligibility gates and mandatory CPF refund netting.  
> **Statutory Authorities**: Housing and Development Act 1959, Women's Charter 1961 (2020 Rev Ed), Central Provident Fund Act 1953, Stamp Duties Act 1929.  
> **Related Protocols**: [DEC-449: Matrimonial Asset Netting](../decision/DEC-449-matrimonial-asset-netting.md), [STR-450: Transient Leverage to Binding Terms Converter](../strategy/STR-450-leverage-to-terms-converter.md)

## 1. Retention Eligibility Gate (The Hard Wall)

Under HDB regulations, a divorced party cannot simply "buy out" or take over the flat unless they independently satisfy an established HDB eligibility scheme. 

If neither party meets any of the criteria below, **the flat CANNOT be retained solo by either party**. It must be sold on the open market (if MOP is met) or surrendered to HDB.

| Scheme / Pathway | Eligibility Criteria | Flat Type Allowed | Retention Feasibility |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Single Singapore Citizen Scheme (SSCS)** | • Singapore Citizen (SC)<br>• Age >= 35 years old at date of divorce order | • Resale flat of any size (standard)<br>• 2-room Flexi BTO (new) | 🟢 **Permitted** if age and citizenship conditions are strictly met. |
| **Child Care & Control Scheme** | • Sole or shared care-and-control of child under 21 years old<br>• Parent must have physical custody/care | • Existing flat retained regardless of size | 🟢 **Permitted** for custodial parent with care and control. |
| **Public Scheme (with New Eligible Co-owner)** | • Inclusion of parents (SC/SPR) or new eligible SC family member as co-owner/tenant | • Existing flat retained | 🟢 **Permitted** subject to HDB approval and co-owner eligibility. |
| **Non-Citizen / Under-35 Solo (No Child)** | • SPR / Foreigner OR Singapore Citizen under 35 years old without child care-and-control | None | 🔴 **PROHIBITED** (Flat must be sold or surrendered; no exceptions). |

```
RETENTION GATE CHECK:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Does at least one party qualify under:                  │
│ 1. SSCS (SC AND Age >= 35)?                             │
│ 2. Child Care & Control (Custody of child < 21)?        │
│ 3. Public Scheme (Join with eligible parents/family)?   │
└────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                             │
              ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
              ▼                             ▼
           [ YES ]                       [ NO ]
    Eligible party may buy        Neither party can retain flat.
    out ex-spouse's share         Must execute Pre-MOP Disposal
    (subject to bank/CPF loan)    Hierarchy or Open-Market Sale.
```

## 2. Pre-MOP Disposal Hierarchy

If divorce occurs **before** the 5-year (or 10-year for Prime/Plus) Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) is fulfilled, standard open-market resale is blocked by law (Housing and Development Act 1959, s 49A).

The options must be evaluated in strict priority order:

| Rank | Option | Mechanism | Financial & Strategic Impact |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1** | **Appeal to HDB for Early Open-Market Sale** | Joint appeal to HDB citing exceptional circumstances (e.g. divorce with mutual consent, insolvency, medical crisis). | 🟢 **Optimal Outcome**: Allows sale on open market at fair market value. HDB approval is strictly discretionary and granted only on strong grounds. |
| **2** | **Deed of Separation (Joint Hold to MOP)** | Execute binding Deed of Separation; maintain joint legal ownership until MOP expires, then execute open-market sale. | 🟡 **Controlled Risk**: Preserves equity upside. Requires room rental to service mortgage and strict behavioral compliance (no remarriage or property acquisition during hold). |
| **3** | **Compulsory Surrender to HDB** | Surrender flat directly back to HDB at prevailing compensation price determined by HDB valuation. | 🔴 **Severe Capital Loss**: Buyback price is discounted (often purchase price minus administrative fees/depreciation). CPF grants are clawed back. Cash proceeds typically zero or negative. |
| **4** | **Booking Cancellation / Forfeiture (Pre-Key Collection)** | If keys have not been collected: terminate HDB BTO contract. | 🔴 **Total Sunk Loss**: 5% deposit/downpayment forfeited to HDB. CPF housing grants returned with accrued interest. 1-year debarment penalty from BTO application. |

## 3. Rental Compliance During MOP

Renting out an HDB flat while subject to MOP is tightly policed by HDB under the Housing and Development Act. Breaches result in compulsory acquisition and forfeiture of the flat.

| Rental Dimension | Bedroom Rental (During MOP) | Whole-Unit Rental (During MOP) | Whole-Unit Rental (Post-MOP) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Legality** | 🟢 **Permitted** (3-room and larger flats) | 🔴 **STRICTLY ILLEGAL** | 🟢 **Permitted** (Standard flats only) |
| **Owner Residency Mandate** | At least 1 registered owner **must physically reside** in the flat continuously. | N/A (Cannot rent out whole flat). | Owner not required to reside. |
| **Minimum Tenancy Period** | 6 months (Short-term / Airbnb prohibited). | N/A | 6 months. |
| **Occupancy Cap (Total Pax)** | • 3-room: Max 4 occupants<br>• 4-room+: Max 6 occupants<br>*(Includes owners + tenants)* | 0 tenants | • 3-room: Max 4 occupants<br>• 4-room+: Max 6 occupants |
| **Citizenship / Flat Type Restrictions** | Available to SC and SPR owners. 1-room and 2-room flats prohibited from renting bedrooms. | Prohibited for all flat types. | • Singapore Citizen owners ONLY.<br>• SPR-only households prohibited.<br>• **Prime & Plus models STRICTLY PROHIBITED** from renting whole unit even post-MOP. |

> ⚠️ **Ghost Landlord Trap**: Registering room rental while locking the master bedroom and living elsewhere is classified by HDB as unauthorized whole-unit subletting. HDB conducts unannounced physical inspections. Penalty: Revocation of lease and compulsory flat repossession.

## 4. Divorce Grounds Menu (Women's Charter 1961)

Under the Women's Charter 1961 (2020 Rev Ed), divorce proceedings must navigate the 3-year statutory marriage bar and specific legal grounds.

### 4.1 The 3-Year Marriage Bar (Section 94)
- **Standard Rule (s 94(1))**: No petition for divorce may be filed within the first 3 years of marriage.
- **Exceptions (s 94(2))**: The plaintiff may apply for leave to file within 3 years ONLY by proving:
  1. **Exceptional Hardship** suffered by the plaintiff; OR
  2. **Exceptional Depravity** on the part of the defendant.
  *(High evidential threshold; ordinary domestic distress or standard infidelity does not suffice).*

### 4.2 Facts Proving Irretrievable Breakdown (Section 95(3))
If the marriage is > 3 years old, the plaintiff must prove one of five statutory facts:

| Fact | Statutory Provision | Evidential Requirement | Strategic Note |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Adultery** | s 95(3)(a) | Proof of sexual intercourse + intolerable to live together. Co-named co-defendant required. | Evidentially burdensome; private investigator reports required. |
| **Unreasonable Behaviour** | s 95(3)(b) | Defendant behaved such that plaintiff cannot reasonably be expected to live with them. | Most common fault-based ground; covers domestic abuse, financial ruin, addiction. |
| **Desertion** | s 95(3)(c) | Continuous desertion for at least 2 years without consent. | Requires total physical and intentional abandonment. |
| **Separation (3 Years)** | s 95(3)(d) | Continuous separation for 3 years **with defendant's consent**. | Cleanest non-fault route if both parties agree. |
| **Separation (4 Years)** | s 95(3)(e) | Continuous separation for 4 years **without consent**. | Unilateral route; defendant consent not required. |

### 4.3 Divorce by Mutual Agreement (DMA) (Section 95A — Effective 1 July 2024)
- **Mechanism**: Both spouses jointly agree that the marriage has broken down irretrievably without citing fault-based facts.
- **Requirements**:
  1. Detailed written agreement stating reasons for breakdown.
  2. Concrete agreed terms on ancillary matters: child custody/care/control, maintenance, and division of matrimonial home.
  3. Evidence that reconciliation efforts were attempted and failed.

## 5. CPF Netting Rule & Statutory Refund Mandate

Under the Central Provident Fund Act 1953 (s 15 & s 27) and HDB sales directives, proceeds from the disposal of an HDB flat **must be refunded to the CPF accounts of both parties before any cash can be disbursed**.

```
PROCEEDS DISTRIBUTION PRIORITY ORDER:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Outstanding Mortgage Loan (Bank / HDB Loan)          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. CPF Principal Used (Party A + Party B)               │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. CPF Accrued Interest @ 2.5% p.a. (Party A + Party B) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. CPF Housing Grants Received + Accrued Interest       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) (if sold within 3 years)   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 6. Legal Fees + Estate Agent Commission (2% + GST)      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 7. BALANCE = Net Cash Proceeds (Split per Court Order)  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Statutory Netting Principles:
1. **Accrued Interest Compounding**: CPF principal withdrawn attracts interest at the prevailing CPF Ordinary Account rate (2.5% per annum, compounded annually).
2. **Refund Irrevocability**: CPF refunds cannot be waived, reduced, or gifted to the spouse via private contract. The CPF Board holds a first statutory charge over the property.
3. **Negative Cash Proceeds Protection**: If market sale price is at or above HDB market valuation, but proceeds are insufficient to cover full CPF principal + interest after clearing the mortgage loan, neither party is required to top up the CPF shortfall with cash. However, **zero cash proceeds** will be realized.
4. **Below-Market Sale Penalty**: If the flat is sold below HDB market valuation, the shortfall in CPF refund must be made good in cash by the selling owners.

| Current Status | Years Married | MOP Fulfilled? | Children < 21? | Recommended Tactical Sequence |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **BTO Booked (No Keys)** | Any | No | Any | Cancel booking. Forfeit 5% deposit. Split liability 50/50 via private agreement. |
| **Occupied (Pre-MOP)** | < 3 Years | No | No | 1. Execute Deed of Separation.<br>2. Rent bedrooms legally to service loan.<br>3. Hold until Year 3 → File DMA (s 95A).<br>4. Sell on open market at Year 5 (MOP). |
| **Occupied (Pre-MOP)** | >= 3 Years | No | Yes | 1. File DMA or s 95(3)(b).<br>2. Transfer flat to custodial parent if financially capable, OR joint hold to MOP.<br>3. Offset child maintenance against housing payments. |
| **Occupied (Post-MOP)** | >= 3 Years | Yes | No (Age < 35) | 1. Sell immediately on open market.<br>2. Execute [DEC-449] netting calculator.<br>3. Net cash split per financial contribution matrix. |
| **Occupied (Post-MOP)** | >= 3 Years | Yes | Yes OR Age >= 35 | 1. Value flat at independent market appraisal.<br>2. Eligible party buys out ex-spouse via CPF/loan refinance.<br>3. Ex-spouse CPF principal + interest refunded in full. |

## 7. Anti-Patterns & Disallowed Advice

| Anti-Pattern | Fatal Flaw | Correct Advisory Directive |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **"I will take over the BTO solo" (Under 35, No Kids)** | Legally impossible under HDB SSCS rules. HDB will reject transfer. | Flag the SSCS Hard Wall immediately. Require co-ownership with parents or execute sale/surrender. |
| **Quoting Gross Upside as Net Profit** | Ignoring S$50K–S$150K in CPF accrued interest, mortgage balance, agent commission (2.18%), and legal fees. | Mandatory execution of [DEC-449 Matrimonial Asset Netting Calculator](../decision/DEC-449-matrimonial-asset-netting.md). |
| **Recommending Whole-Unit Subletting During MOP** | Criminal violation of Housing and Development Act. Risk of total flat forfeiture without compensation. | Mandate registered bedroom rental only, with at least one owner maintaining verified continuous physical residence. |
| **Ignoring Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD)** | Disposing within 3 years triggers 12% (Yr 1), 8% (Yr 2), or 4% (Yr 3) duty on full market value. | Factor SSD into holding period trade-off analysis before initiating early sale. |
| **Premature Public Feud / Social Media Leak** | Destroys settlement leverage, invites defamation suits, and triggers bank/HDB scrutiny. | Invoke [STR-450 Leverage to Terms Converter](../strategy/STR-450-leverage-to-terms-converter.md). Maintain absolute silence until binding Deed of Separation is executed. |
